# 🌿 Canopy — Personalized Digital Garden Companion

An empathetic, AI-driven full-stack web application that transforms routine houseplant care into an engaging digital experience by merging virtual pet dynamics, real-world plant diagnostics, and a context-aware botanical AI assistant.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🏡 Environment Parameterization Wizard**
  Assess your local growing conditions (humidity, sunlight harshness, peak daylight hours, rainfall profile, and placement zone) to receive real-time plant compatibility matching.

- **🪴 Interactive 2D Virtual Garden**
  A dynamic canvas with swappable spatial zones (Balcony, Terrace, Indoor Shelf) powered by interactive Rive plant avatars that react visually to your care routine.

- **📊 Automated Emotional Dynamics Engine**
  Integrated habit tracker via the Garden Log that evaluates 7-day care consistency to drive real-time virtual plant sentiment:
  - ✨ **Shiny / Happy:** High care consistency (>85% completion rate)
  - 💃 **Dancing:** Daily maintenance streak milestones
  - 🥀 **Sad / Drooping:** Unmet care tasks over 48 hours
  - 🍂 **Browning / Wilted:** Extended neglect over 5+ consecutive days

- **🛡️ Empathetic Death & Revive Mechanism**
  Zero harsh deletion penalties for wilted plants. Offers a recovery choice between:
  - ✂️ **Prune & Restart:** Resets growth state to *Sprout/Seedling* with a clean health record.
  - ♻️️ **Convert to Compost:** Recycles the wilted plant into a *Compost Shield* (1-day streak-freeze safeguard).

- **🤖 Rootly AI Botanical Assistant**
  A conversational AI chatbot powered by LangChain and OpenAI, automatically injected with your specific garden parameters and historical care logs for personalized troubleshooting.

- **🔍 4-Tab Plant Profile Inspector**
  Select any plant on your canvas to open a detailed status card covering **Watering Schedules**, **Soil Checks**, **Sunlight Exposure**, and **Health/Illness Logs**.

- **🛒 Gardening Essentials Shelf**
  Curated marketplace featuring direct links to essential tools, organic fertilizers, soil mixes, and care accessories.

## 🔧 Requirements

- **Node.js:** `v18.17.0` – `v20.x`
- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- **AI Access:** OpenAI API Key (for Rootly AI integration)
- **Browser:** Any modern WebGL-enabled browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox)
